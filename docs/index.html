How to use CyberLearn

  • On your own: read a lesson, take the quiz, then try the matching challenge set.
  • In a group or class: lessons print cleanly as handouts (use the print button on any lesson).
  • Not sure where to begin? Lessons 1 to 3 need no technical background. Read Law and ethics before you try Pen Testing: it explains where the legal line is.
